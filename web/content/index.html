03 · Content Enrichment

Inventory that
repairs itself.

Supplier feeds arrive with holes: no description, no amenities, nothing to rank on. A scanner finds them, a queue distributes the work, and a worker writes copy grounded strictly in facts already on the listing. No invented pools, no invented views.

Press reset to put a slice of the catalogue back into a broken state, then run a scan and watch the queue drain. Click any repaired listing to see its before, its after, and which path produced it.

Grounded, then checked

The prompt receives only the listing's own fields and is told not to invent. The reply is then validated in code: every original amenity must survive, and at most three may be added.

Safe to replay

A worker claims a listing with a compare-and-set in SQL, so two workers racing means one wins and the other cleanly skips. Overlapping scans are deduplicated at enqueue time.

Failure stays visible

Transient errors release the claim so the queue can retry with backoff. Exhausted retries park the listing as failed, where this dashboard shows it rather than losing it silently.
